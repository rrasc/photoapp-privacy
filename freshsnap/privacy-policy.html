Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 23, 2026

FreshSnap ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Local Data: All food inventory data (item names, expiry dates, quantities, storage locations) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data.

Photos: When you use the AI scan feature, food item photos are sent to Google's Gemini AI service for analysis. Photos are processed in real-time and are not stored on any server after processing.

Purchase Data: In-app purchases are processed by Google Play and RevenueCat. We do not store your payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide AI-powered food scanning and identification
  • To track your food inventory and expiry dates locally
  • To process in-app purchases and manage scan credits
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini AI — For food item recognition and label scanning
  • RevenueCat — For managing in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • Expo/EAS — For app updates and distribution

4. Data Storage

Your food inventory data is stored entirely on your device using local storage. No account is required to use the app. If you uninstall the app, your data will be permanently deleted.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Photos sent for AI analysis are not retained after processing.

6. Children's Privacy

FreshSnap is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. All data is stored locally on your device, minimizing exposure to external threats.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.
